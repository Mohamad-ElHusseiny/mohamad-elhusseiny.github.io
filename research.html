RESEARCH OVERVIEW

Optimizing Image Resolution and Prompting for Cost-Efficient Multimodal LLMs.

Student research under Professor Fouad Trad at Lebanese American University, investigating accuracy and resource use in scientific visual question answering.

The research question

How much visual information does a model need to answer a scientific question? I study how image resolution, caption availability, and prompting affect answer quality and input-token usage.

What I work on

Our work includes experiments with local vision-language models and matched cloud API comparisons. I contribute to experimental pipelines, preprocessing verification, resource accounting, and analysis of model responses.

The evaluation considers perception, hypothesis generation, and experiment proposal separately, without assuming a fixed difficulty ranking between them.

What the work motivates

Our observations suggest that reducing image resolution can lower input-token usage, while its effect on accuracy varies across questions and task categories. This motivates investigating adaptive visual budgets; it does not establish that uniform resizing always fails or that one resolution is optimal.

A direction I would like to explore

I am interested in implementing and benchmarking a lightweight, question-conditioned resolution selector. A prospective study would compare it with fixed-scale baselines on held-out image groups while accounting for selection overhead, latency, and task-level accuracy.

This is a prospective research interest, not a completed method or a claim of novelty.

How I approach research

  • Verify actual model preprocessing and token usage.
  • Preserve reproducible experimental records and separate observations from interpretations.
  • Consider shared-image grouping when evaluating uncertainty.
  • Report limitations and distinguish token counts from measured financial or electricity savings.

This page provides a high-level overview. Unpublished experimental grids, numerical results, protocols, source images, and lab data are not distributed through this portfolio.
