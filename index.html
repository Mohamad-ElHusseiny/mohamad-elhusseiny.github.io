SPATIAL DATA / RESEARCH INTERNSHIP

DynaCITY at AUB MSFEA

Maroun Semaan Faculty of Engineering and Architecture · American University of Beirut

During my June–August 2026 research internship at the American University of Beirut, I worked on DynaCITY in MSFEA’s Vision and Robotics Lab. I built a PostgreSQL/PostGIS city model, a FastAPI backend, and a browser-based 3D viewer.

Engineering the experiment

I designed controlled spatial-index benchmarks using pre-registered hypotheses, result-equivalence checks, and interleaved measurements. The work compared custom quadtree point lookup with PostgreSQL spatial indexing under controlled conditions.

Indexing performance depends on the setup and query type. The project card uses a decorative concept render.

PostgreSQL / PostGISFastAPIPythonSpatial indexing
RETRIEVAL / AI SYSTEMS INTERNSHIP

OGERO Chatbot

During my June–September 2026 internship at OGERO, I worked on a multilingual chatbot for OGERO using retrieval-augmented generation, PostgreSQL, Redis, and Qdrant.

Reliability beyond retrieval

The work included evaluating retrieval, routing, and tool selection. I corrected missing ticket references using structured source records and checked reference preservation through regression tests.

This is a professional project summary. Private source records, ticket contents, credentials, and internal software are not included in this portfolio.
